# Contributing

Intelligenza Umana starts with a real problem, makes design reasoning visible, examines alternatives and verifies results. AI can assist research and implementation; contributors remain responsible for understanding, reviewing and explaining their work. Read the [manifesto](MANIFESTO.md) and [engineering standard](docs/ENGINEERING_STANDARD.md).

Everyone can read public repositories, discuss ideas in issues, fork a project and propose changes through pull requests. You do not need organization membership to contribute this way. Maintainers review proposed changes; only an authorized maintainer can approve and merge a contribution.

Direct write access and maintainer duties are reserved for organization members or people explicitly granted the relevant repository permissions. To request organization membership, email intelligenzaumana.lab@gmail.com with your GitHub username, areas of interest and a short description of how you would like to participate. Membership and additional permissions are granted based on project needs.

1. Check existing issues and the [project registry](docs/PROJECT_REGISTRY.md). Describe the problem, context, evidence and expected outcome.
2. Discuss substantial or architectural changes before implementation.
3. Fork the repository, make a focused change and open a pull request. Include verification commands, actual results, relevant security and data-integrity considerations, and known limitations.
4. Respond to review and improve the change together. Passing checks alone do not constitute approval.

There is no seniority threshold. Ask questions or request clarification at any point. Disclose material AI assistance and verify AI-generated work. See the [definition of done](docs/DEFINITION_OF_DONE.md) and [code of conduct](community/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

**Do not post vulnerabilities or secrets in public issues.** Follow the private reporting instructions in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
