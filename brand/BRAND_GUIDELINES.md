# Brand guidelines

The approved identity is the human-centred emblem: an open hand supporting a Vitruvian-inspired human figure framed by mathematical geometry, open arcs and a four-point star. The modern sans-serif wordmark is approved in monochrome light and dark variants.

The canonical assets, usage notes and symbolism are maintained in [assets/brand](../assets/brand/README.md). Logo 02 (the former IU monogram) is retired and must not be used in new materials.

Verify 16/32 px readability, circular crop, monochrome reproduction and similarities before deployment. No identity changes without approval.
