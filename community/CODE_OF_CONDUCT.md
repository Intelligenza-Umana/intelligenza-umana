# Code of conduct
Critique ideas, not people. Reason, verify sources and acknowledge mistakes and uncertainty. No harassment, discrimination, threats, pressure, disclosure of others' data or spam. Respect privacy and consent. Report concerns privately to the founder at intelligenzaumana.lab@gmail.com; do not post personal or sensitive details publicly. The founder reviews reports as capacity allows, hears the people involved when appropriate and may restrict access to protect the community. No response time is guaranteed. Anyone may leave freely.
