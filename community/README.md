# IU Community
An early-stage open-source community welcoming collaboration through issues, proposals, pull requests and peer review. The foundation is maintained by the founder; participation is open and review depends on available capacity. No seniority requirement; understanding, evidence and respect are mandatory. No legal association or employment relationship.
