# Roadmap
- Foundation: maintain governance and standards; maintain the security mailbox and verify reply delivery. The tree and Markdown link paths were audited at commit 765c1de.
- Extensions: verify installation, tests, licenses, English documentation and releases for applicable projects.
- Open-source readiness: CONTRIBUTING, conduct, SECURITY, PR criteria, CI and issues; maintain the GitHub organization and verify access.
- Research: real problems, alternatives, gaps, RFCs and verifiable prototypes.
- Flagship: scope reproducible AI-agent assurance experiments and enable modular contributions.
- Community: gradual collaboration based on interest and maintenance capacity.
- Web: catalog, documentation, releases and contributions.
No artificial deadlines or social media obligations. Editorial strategy retired; history remains in Git.

Uncommitted proposals and deferred ideas are recorded separately in [Future ideas](../ideas/README.md). Inclusion does not create a roadmap commitment.

## Shared operational priorities

Keep beginner-friendly issues discoverable, verify releases and installations with reproducible evidence, maintain access controls and private reporting, and check integrations when infrastructure changes. Grow community infrastructure only when actual demand warrants it; see [future ideas](../ideas/README.md).
